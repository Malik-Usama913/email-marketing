# Ijadi — Email Brand Execution Guide

A condensed guide for designers and copywriters creating promotional, onboarding, update, and customer communication emails.

### Brand Overview:

Ijadi builds AI voice employees for residential service businesses. The platform captures missed inbound calls, automates outbound follow-up, and reactivates dormant leads — turning revenue leakage into booked jobs.

### Brand Personality:

Calm authority • Strategic • Structured • Intelligent • Operationally focused • Results-driven

### Core Messaging:

Focus on revenue systems, operational control, predictable pipelines, and captured opportunities — not generic AI hype or automation buzzwords.

### Voice & Tone:

**We sound like:** Structured, confident, calm, precise, customer-aware.

**We avoid:** Overhype, emotional exaggeration, corporate jargon, flashy startup language, or generic AI terminology.

### Communication Style

Short to medium sentences. One idea at a time. Structure messaging as: Problem → Insight → Solution. Speak like an infrastructure strategist, not a marketer.

### Writing Rules

- Keep paragraphs short
- Use strong operational outcomes
- Focus on missed revenue and pipeline control
- Use direct CTAs
- Mobile-first formatting
- Prioritize clarity over cleverness

**Preferred Language Use:** Predictable revenue, capture, system, pipeline, follow-up, control, booked jobs, infrastructure, AI employees.

**Avoid:** Revolutionary, AI magic, cheap, leads service, automation tool.

# Email Design & Campaign Standards

### Visual Direction

Modern SaaS aesthetic with dark backgrounds, premium spacing, minimal layouts, and clean hierarchy. Use vivid blue and neon cyan accents with subtle glow effects and UI-inspired visuals.

### Brand Colors:

| Role       | Color     |
|------------|-----------|
| Primary    | `#766CF2` |
| Secondary  | `#00F5FF` |
| Text       | `#DEDEDE` |
| Background | `#bbbbbb` |

**Gradients:**

1. `#766CF2` → `#3222E7` (Blue/Purple)
2. `#766CF2` → `#00F5FF` (Purple/Cyan)

### Typography

**Headings:** Montserrat

**Body:** Outfit

Maintain clean spacing, bold hierarchy, and highly readable layouts.

### Email Types

- Product updates
- Promotional campaigns
- Feature launches
- Customer education
- Reactivation campaigns
- Seasonal demand campaigns

### CTA Style

Use direct, action-focused CTAs:

- Book a Demo
- Activate AI Employee
- Recover Missed Calls
- View System

Avoid vague CTAs like “Click Here” or “Learn More.”

### Headline Examples

**Good:**

- Stop Losing Revenue to Missed Calls
- Your Pipeline Shouldn’t Depend on Staffing
- More Leads Won’t Fix Weak Follow-Up

**Avoid:**

- Revolutionary AI Automation Platform
- The Future of Sales Technology

### Non-Negotiables

- Always maintain a premium SaaS look
- Avoid cluttered layouts
- Focus on outcomes, not technical features
- Keep tone calm and authoritative
- Every email should feel operationally intelligent and strategically structured

# Email Layout

### 1. Padding & Margin Rules

- **Global Email Padding:** 16px inner padding for mobile, 32px for desktop containers.
- **Section Padding:** Top/bottom 24px (mobile) → 48px (desktop) between major sections.
- **Text Blocks:** 12–16px spacing between paragraphs; 8px line-height for headings.
- **CTA Buttons:** 16px vertical + 24px horizontal padding; minimum touch target 44x44px (mobile).

### 2. Responsive Brand Email Guidelines

- All emails must be mobile-first, scaling gracefully for tablets and desktop.
- **Fluid layouts:** Use percentage widths, max-width 600px container.
- **Flexible images:** Width: 100%, height: auto.
- **Font scaling:** Headings: 20–28px (mobile), 28–36px (desktop); Body: 14–16px mobile, 16–18px desktop.
- Stack columns on mobile; side-by-side on desktop (media queries).

### 3. SaaS Platform Styling Considerations

- Emails should feel like a premium SaaS product (clean hierarchy, spacing, structure).
- Use dark-mode friendly layouts with high-contrast accents (Vivid Blue `#5345EC`, Neon Cyan `#00D3DB`).
- Avoid cluttered layouts and overly complex graphics. Keep UI-inspired minimalism.
- **Interactive elements:** Buttons and links styled like SaaS dashboards (rounded corners, subtle shadow, hover effect).

### 4. Emoji Usage Instead of Icons

- Replace traditional icons in emails with relevant emojis to convey meaning:
- Example:
  - 📞 — Call / missed call
  - 🔄 — Follow-up / reactivation
  - ✅ — Booked job / success
  - ⚡ — Speed / quick response
  - 🏗️ — Operational work
  - 💼 — Business / system
- Avoid stock icons; emojis improve mobile readability and visual appeal while keeping SaaS aesthetic.

### 5. Additional Brand Consistency Rules

- Maintain calm authority, structured clarity, and operational precision in all email copy.
- CTA language must remain actionable and direct:
  - **Good:** “Activate AI Employee” / “Recover Missed Calls” / “Book a Demo”
  - **Avoid:** “Click Here” / “Learn More” / overhyped terms.
- Retain premium spacing, readable typography (Montserrat/Outfit), hierarchy, and clarity across all devices.
