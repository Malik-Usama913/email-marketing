# Ijadi Features

*AI voice employees for home services, insurance, and real estate businesses.*

> **We sell jobs, not software.** Ijadi is an outcome-driven AI operator — more conversations, more booked appointments, without hiring more staff. Capture every call, book every job.

---

## AI Voice Agents

### Your AI employee, not just an AI tool

Ijadi's conversational AI agents ("Sarah" and "Lucas") work as real team members — answering inbound calls, running outbound dialing campaigns at volume, qualifying leads, and booking appointments in first-person conversation, just like a human rep would.

### Built for your industry

Purpose-built conversation flows for Insurance, HVAC, Roofing, and Real Estate — covering the moments that cost businesses the most revenue: missed inbound calls, cold outbound prospecting, and reactivating a dormant customer database.

### Live call transfers

Qualified leads can be transferred live from the AI agent to a human closer mid-call, so hot conversations never go cold waiting on a callback.

### Conversation memory

Turn on Memory for any agent and it will recall prior conversations with the same contact on future calls — no more asking customers to repeat themselves. Off by default, so it's opt-in wherever repeat-caller context adds value (support, relationship-based sales).

### Inbound and outbound, one system

Every AI Voice interaction — inbound or outbound — flows into the same customer record, so your team always has the full picture regardless of which way the call came in.

---

## Call Intelligence & Reliability

### AI call summaries

After every completed call, Ijadi automatically generates a plain-language summary — who the contact is, what was discussed, and the outcome — alongside the full conversation, so teams never have to relisten to a call to know what happened.

### Full call transcripts & recordings

Every call is recorded and transcribed automatically, giving businesses a searchable record of every conversation for training, quality review, and dispute resolution.

### Automatic call disposition tagging

Ijadi's AI assistant automatically tags the outcome of every call (e.g. DNC, Voicemail, Not Interested, Call Back, Follow Up, lead won) so teams get accurate, up-to-date call outcomes without manual logging.

## Compliance & Trust

### Compliance by design

Every AI call opens with a mandatory disclosure — *"This is an AI calling on behalf of \[Business Name\]"* — satisfying TCPA and FCC telemarketing requirements out of the box.

### TCPA-compliant calling hours

Set the exact hours calls and texts go out so your business only reaches customers within the legal calling window (8am–9pm), protecting you from compliance violations automatically.

### Do-Not-Call (DNC) and opt-out handling

DNC contacts are automatically respected — messaging is blocked and clearly flagged wherever a DNC contact appears, and opt-outs are honored platform-wide.

---

## Scheduling & Calendar

### Automatic appointment booking (Cal.com integration)

Connect Cal.com once and Ijadi's AI agent books, reschedules, and cancels appointments automatically — synced in real time to Google Calendar, Outlook, and more. No more phone tag or double-bookings. It also respect your blocked time.

### Every booking channel in one place

Appointments booked by AI Voice (inbound or outbound), SMS (inbound or outbound), a shareable booking link, or manual entry are all captured with complete details — booking reference, meeting link, exact times, and attendees — so staff can manage rescheduling, cancellations, and no-shows from one portal.

### Shareable booking links

Generate a booking link for prospects to schedule themselves, with bookings, reschedules, and cancellations syncing automatically and securely.

## Usage & Billing Visibility

### Usage tracking widget

A simple percentage-dial view (accessible from your profile) shows Minutes used and SMS sent for the current period, so you always know what's left on your plan.

## Campaign Controls

### Pause & resume calling campaigns

Instantly stop all calls in a campaign with one click — nothing is lost, contacts and settings stay intact — then resume with a single click. Ideal for holidays or busy stretches.

### Time zone settings

Choose your preferred time zone in Profile Settings so every date, timestamp, and scheduled activity displays in local time.

### Browser Dialer

Dial straight from the browser in Auto Dial or Manual Dial mode, with call recordings available for every manual call.

---

## Messaging & Outreach

### Group messaging with visibility

Send messages to multiple contacts at once and see exactly what the campaign did — how many messages went out, how many replies came back, and what conversations it started.

### Bulk contact upload

Import contact lists in bulk, with duplicate contacts cleared in a single click instead of one at a time.
